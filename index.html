Ridge Pack

You are a lone gray wolf in a wilderness 3 kilometers across: more than half a million tiles of hollows, lakes, rivers and ridgelines, generated fresh for every pack. Sneak up on deer, dig a den, then go underground to carve out chambers and choose how it looks. Once your den is big and holds 10 meat, you can raise pups. Pups that survive three dawns join your pack and hunt beside you.

Black bears hold the woods and raid poorly guarded dens, but they warn before they charge and give up fast once you put distance or water between you. Hunters in blaze orange work the open grass by day. Stay in the trees and they will struggle to see you.
